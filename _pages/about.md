I am a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at [Florida State University](https://www.fsu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Guang Wang](http://guangwang.me/#/home) in the [Data, Computing, and Society (DCS) Lab](http://guangwang.me/#/student). Before joining FSU, I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from [Southeast University](https://www.seu.edu.cn/english/), where I worked with Prof. [Shuai Wang](https://cs.seu.edu.cn/shuaiwang/main.htm) and Prof. [Tian He](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hc1m_BQAAAAJ) on trustworthy decision-making. During my master’s studies, I also collaborated closely with Prof. [Desheng Zhang](https://people.cs.rutgers.edu/~dz220/) on smart logistics.

My research develops **spatiotemporal intelligence for generation, prediction, and decision-making**. Currently, I am interested in **diffusion-based time-series generation**, with a focus on fidelity, diversity, efficiency, and especially extreme fidelity. I value both methodological research and real-world deployment, with applications including transportation, energy, healthcare, and urban systems. Beyond my primary research, I contribute to collaborative projects on uncertainty quantification and LLM reasoning.

Recurring threads in my work:

- **Spatiotemporal Generative Modeling.** Covering temporal data generation ([E4GEN](/publications/#jiang2026e4gen), [SynEHR](/publications/#li2026synehr), [SynEnergy](/publications/#jiang2026synenergy), [MBDiff](/publications/#xu2026mbdiff)) and human mobility and activity trace generation ([SynHAT](/publications/#jiang2026synhat), [GeoGen](/publications/#xu2026geogen), [MobiDiff](/publications/#xu2026mobidiff), [ZeroHAT](/publications/#xu2026zerohat)).
- **Uncertainty Quantification.** Covering uncertainty-aware spatiotemporal prediction ([HealthMamba](/publications/#yu2026healthmamba), [EnergyMamba](/publications/#yu2026energymamba), [UQGNN](/publications/#yu2025uqgnn)) and uncertainty quantification for LLM reasoning ([TrAC](/publications/#yu2026trac), [SymboUQ](/publications/#yu2026symbouq), [ChainUQ](/publications/#yu2026chainuq)).
- **Trustworthy Decision-Making.** Covering trustworthy dispatch and fleet management ([UrbanHuRo](/publications/#jiang2026urbanhuro), [HCRide](/publications/#jiang2025hcriede), [FairMove](/publications/#wang2023fairmove), [FairCod](/publications/#jiang2023faircod), [MSFair](/publications/#cao2022multisided)) and resilient power system management ([EPOPR](/publications/#jiang2025upro)).
- **Additional Research Directions.** Including POI recommendation ([EviRec](/publications/#xu2026evirec)) and speech emotion recognition ([JDAR](/publications/#zhang2021crosscorpus)).

My work has appeared at NeurIPS, ACM SIGKDD, IJCAI, AAAI, ICRA, ACM IMWUT/UbiComp, ACM SIGSPATIAL, ACM CIKM, SDM, and ICASSP. I have received the **Dean’s Award for Doctoral Excellence**, the **Graduate Student Research Award**, the **Naaman Franklin Faile Jr. Graduate Fellowship**, and the **Ted and Syauchen Baker Scholarship** from Florida State University. I have also received travel grants from IJCAI, SDM, and KDD. I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations — feel free to reach out.
